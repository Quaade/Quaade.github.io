About

Designing games
from the ground up

I'm a game designer who implements his own systems, based in Copenhagen, Denmark. I specialise in melee combat and character mechanics — frame data, hitboxes, cancel windows, state machines — and the designer-facing tooling that turns balance into a fast iteration loop instead of a rebuild.

I've shipped thirteen playable characters on a live competitive fighting game and written the C# underneath them. Over the past decade I've worked across the full production pipeline, from concept and blockout through to live patches, in indie studios, on AAA projects, and independently.

My primary engine is Unity with C#, and I currently build in Godot; I'm also proficient in Unreal. I care about craft, iteration, and how the game feels in the hands above all else.
